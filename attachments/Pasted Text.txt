2) Background / screen-off

   - Keep one playing element; warm() preload stays muted, no play(), must not take Media Session.
   - Keep playsinline on the main element.

   - Sync store status if the element pauses from an OS interruption (phone call).
   - No service worker required for v1 unless one already exists and helps; don’t invent a big SW.
   - Comment: iOS Safari limits some headset gestures; Chrome/Brave/Android are the primary AirPods-style win. Media Session attaches after first user-gesture unlocked play — don’t break enter-gate / autoplay lamp.
